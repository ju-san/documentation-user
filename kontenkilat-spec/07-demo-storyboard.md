# 07 — Demo Video Storyboard (60 Detik)

Demo utama untuk: landing page hero, ProductHunt gallery, Meta Ads, TikTok pinned.

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920, MP4
**Durasi:** 60 detik
**Audio:** Voiceover Bahasa Indonesia + background music + sound effects
**Bahasa:** Indonesia (with optional English subtitle version)

## Konsep Cerita

**Premise:** Owner UMKM frustrated bikin video manual → discover KontenKilat → output stunning dalam 60 detik → ROI obvious.

**Style:** Real-life mockup + screen recording + UI animation. Bukan full animation (terasa lebih authentic).

## Production Notes

### Filming Requirements

**Talent needed:**
- 1 owner UMKM (cewek, 25-35, hijab atau casual representative target)
- Optional: 1 cameo customer (for scene 8 visual)

**Locations:**
- Home office / kafe (untuk scene 1-2 frustrated owner)
- Studio bersih (untuk close-up tangan + product)

**Props:**
- Laptop dengan tabs ready (CapCut, Canva, Google)
- Jam dinding visible
- Sample product (skincare untuk match demo prompt)
- Phone dengan IG/TikTok app

### Screen Recording

**Recording setup:**
- Use Loom atau OBS untuk record KontenKilat actual usage
- Resolution: 1920×1080 source, crop ke 9:16
- Smooth cursor animation (use CleanShot atau cursor highlighter)
- Show UI dengan all loading states properly (not skipped)

### Voiceover

**Voice talent options:**
1. **Hire profesional VO** Indonesia (~Rp 500K-1.5jt) — best quality
2. **ElevenLabs** voice yang sama dipakai di product — meta consistency
3. **Founder voice** — authenticity bonus, butuh good mic

**Tone direction:**
- Energetic, friendly, conversational
- Tempo medium-fast (target 160-180 word/min)
- Smile in voice (audiences can hear it)
- Avoid: monotone, overly polished radio voice

**Script word count:** ~90-110 kata (sesuai 60 detik)

### Music

**Genre:** Modern upbeat pop / electronic pop
**Tempo:** 120-130 BPM
**Suggested tracks (royalty-free):**
- Epidemic Sound: "Sunrise" series
- Artlist: "Modern Inspiring Pop"
- Pixabay Music: search "upbeat corporate pop"
- YouTube Audio Library: "Pop > Bright > Inspirational"

**Audio mix:**
- Music: -18dB (background)
- Voiceover: -3dB (foreground)
- SFX: -12dB (accent)

### Color Grading

**Palette:**
- Primary: Teal #00BFA6 (Aiyoo brand)
- Secondary: Purple gradient #6366F1
- Accent: Gold #FFD700 (untuk highlights)
- Dark: #0F172A (background)

**Vibe:** Modern, bright, energetic, tech-forward.

### Typography

**Fonts:**
- Header: Inter Black 96-120px
- Body overlay: Inter Bold 48-72px
- Small text: Inter Medium 32px

**Animation style:**
- Spring entrance (Framer Motion style)
- Quick exit (200ms fade)
- Letter-by-letter typewriter untuk impact text

### Editing Tools

**Recommended:** Premiere Pro atau DaVinci Resolve atau CapCut Pro
**For motion graphics:** After Effects (atau Rive untuk web-native)
**For screen recording polish:** Screen Studio (Mac) atau Camo
