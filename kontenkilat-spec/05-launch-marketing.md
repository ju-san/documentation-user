## Channel Playbook

### TikTok (Primary — sangat strategis untuk tool video)

**Why critical:** Audience kita = TikTok creator. Demo langsung di platform target.

**Strategy:**
- Daily posting
- Every video = demo tool sendiri
- Hook 3 detik: hasil video yang stunning, lalu reveal "ini dibuat AI dalam 30 detik"

**Format yang konsisten work:**

1. **Speed Demo**
   - "Bikin video promo 30 detik tanpa edit"
   - Time-lapse: input prompt → output video
   - Audio: trending "wow" sound

2. **Before/After**
   - Before: amateurish handheld video
   - After: video kita yang professional
   - Same content, totally different vibe

3. **Industry Spesifik**
   - "Video promo skincare dalam 1 menit"
   - "Bikin testimonial customer untuk toko baju"
   - Repeat untuk 20+ industri

4. **POV / Storytelling**
   - "POV: kamu owner UMKM yang nggak bisa edit video"
   - Frustrated → discover tool → relief

5. **Template Reveal**
   - "6 template video buat UMKM 2026"
   - Showcase tiap template singkat

6. **Behind The Scene**
   - "Begini cara kita bikin tool ini"
   - Founder talking, dev process

7. **Tutorial**
   - "3 setting yang bikin video kamu viral"
   - Educational + product showcase

**Hashtag strategy:**
- Mass: #fyp #tiktokindonesia #umkm
- Niche: #tokoonline #aitools #contentcreatorindo
- Brand: #kontenkilat

**Goal bulan 1:** 30 video, 2 viral (>100K view), 15K follower.

## Risk & Plan B

### Risk: TikTok/IG API approval lambat
**Plan B:** Launch dengan "Download & post manual" mode. Tambah copy: "Auto-post coming soon, sedang approval Meta + TikTok."

### Risk: Cost per video > $0.30
**Plan B:**
- Lower res output (720p vs 1080p): saves ~40% Lambda cost
- ElevenLabs caching jika same script
- Pakai Google TTS Indonesia (~10x lebih murah, mungkin sedikit less natural)
- Free tier ketat: 3 video/bln vs 5

### Risk: Output kualitas dibawah ekspektasi
**Plan B:**
- Iterate Remotion compositions intensively minggu pertama
- Pakai stock footage curation (manual approve top keywords)
- Add option "Premium AI footage" via Runway (Pro+ feature, higher cost)

### Risk: Launch flat (<100 signup day 1)
**Plan B:**
- Aggressive cold outreach 200/hari
- Activate all influencer affiliate sekaligus
- Free Pro trial unlimited untuk bulan pertama (extend friction)
- Personal DM ke top 500 prospects

### Risk: Music copyright complaint
**Plan B:**
- HANYA pakai royalty-free dengan license documented
- Takedown procedure jelas
- Force watermark + credit jika needed

### Risk: Pro conversion rendah (<3%)
**Plan B:**
- A/B test pricing (Rp 79K vs 99K vs 129K)
- A/B test free tier limit (3 vs 5 vs 7 video/bulan)
- Add "Pro trial 7 hari" friction-free
- Personal sales follow-up untuk high-intent users
