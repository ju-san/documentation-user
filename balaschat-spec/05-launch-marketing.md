## Channel-by-Channel Playbook

### TikTok (Primary Channel)

**Strategy:** Daily posting, hook 3 detik kuat, demo-driven content.

**Format yang Konsisten Work:**

1. **POV Series**
   - "POV: kamu owner toko, customer chat jam 1 pagi" → demo BalasChat
   - "POV: kamu freelancer, klien ghosting 2 minggu" → demo follow-up template
   - Audio: trending sad-relate sounds

2. **Before vs After**
   - Sebelum: typing manual, frustrated
   - Sesudah: 1 klik, professional reply
   - Audio: transformation trending

3. **Template Series**
   - "Template balas WA customer yang nawar"
   - "Template handle komplain customer marah"
   - "Template follow-up customer yang ghosting"
   - Format: 1 template per video, masing-masing 15-30 detik

4. **Reaction/Commentary**
   - Reaksi terhadap balasan WA yang viral
   - "Balasan toko ini KENAPA sih?"
   - Edukatif + entertaining

5. **Day-in-the-Life**
   - "How I handle 500 WA customer per hari"
   - Authentic, raw, behind-the-scenes

**Hashtag Strategy:**
- Mass: #tokoonline #jualanonline #umkm
- Niche: #reseller #dropshipper #fyp
- Brand: #balaschat #aiyoo

**Posting Schedule:**
- 11:00-13:00 (lunch break audience)
- 19:00-21:00 (after work audience)
- Minggu/Sabtu pagi (weekend scroll)

**Target Bulan 1:** 30 video, 1 video viral (>100K view), 10K follower.

## Risk & Plan B

### Risk: Launch flat (<100 signup day 1)
**Plan B:**
- Push aggressive cold outreach (200 email/hari)
- Activate semua influencer affiliate sekaligus
- Buka diskon Aiyoo "Launch special — Rp 99rb bulan pertama"
- Personal DM ke top 500 prospects manually

### Risk: TikTok account flagged/banned
**Plan B:**
- Multi-account strategy (3 akun aktif paralel)
- Repurpose ke IG Reels + YouTube Shorts
- Hire UGC creator untuk content diversification

### Risk: Cost AI > revenue
**Plan B:**
- Tighten rate limit (3 → 2 anonymous, dst)
- Switch dari Claude Haiku ke yang lebih murah (cek Anthropic pricing terbaru)
- Add "Watch ad for 1 extra generate" mechanism

### Risk: Konversi ke Aiyoo rendah
**Plan B:**
- A/B test placement & copy CTA
- Add demo video Aiyoo di /pro page
- Offer "BalasChat user discount" 50% Aiyoo bulan pertama
- Personal sales follow-up untuk warm leads
